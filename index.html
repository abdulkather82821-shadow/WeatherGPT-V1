Volunteer safely

Coordinate through your district administration, local disaster-management authority or an established relief organization. Do not enter floodwater, damaged buildings or restricted areas; never self-deploy into an active emergency.

Contact official disaster-management channels ↗
🏠

Shelter & accommodation

This app has no verified live shelter directory or bed availability. During an evacuation, follow official instructions and confirm an open relief centre with district officials, your panchayat or local emergency services before travelling.

Check NDMA Sachet updates ↗
🥫

Food & safe water

Keep drinking water in clean, covered containers and use sealed, shelf-stable food. Do not eat food or drink water touched by floodwater; follow local public-health boil-water or distribution instructions. Do not assume a relief kitchen is open unless officials confirm.
